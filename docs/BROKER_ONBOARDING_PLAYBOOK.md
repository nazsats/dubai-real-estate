# Broker Onboarding & Marketing Playbook

How to market the Dubai AI Broker and get real agents testing it. Practical, copy-paste ready.

---

## 0. Prerequisite: make it testable (deploy it)

Brokers won't (and can't) run code. Before any outreach, get a **live URL**:

| Piece | Free host |
|---|---|
| Frontend (Next.js) | **Vercel** |
| Backend (FastAPI) | **Render** or **Railway** |
| Database + storage | **Supabase** |

Then create **one demo account pre-loaded with real listings** (run `import_listings.py`) so a
broker sees value in 10 seconds, plus let them sign up their own agency. *No live URL = no testers.*

---

## 1. Who you're selling to (and what they care about)

- **Individual agents** — care about *time saved* + *more commission*. Busy, on WhatsApp all day, non-technical, skeptical of "another tool."
- **Agency owners/managers** — care about *team productivity*, *lead conversion*, *not losing leads*.

**Sell the outcome, never the tech:** "never forget a follow-up," "pitch any client in seconds,"
"close more with less admin." Nobody buys "AI" — they buy results.

---

## 2. Content strategy

### Three content pillars (rotate these)
1. **Problem/pain** — "Brokers lose deals to slow follow-ups, not bad selling."
2. **Product/wow** — short clips of the AI doing match → pitch → marketing live.
3. **Proof/story** — build-in-public progress, early tester reactions, screenshots.

### Formats that work (in priority order)
1. **45–60s demo videos / reels** (highest reach) — see the LinkedIn Launch Kit.
2. **Carousels** — one feature or one pain point per slide.
3. **Before/after** — "Writing a pitch by hand (2 min) vs with AI (5 sec)."
4. **Single screenshot + a strong hook line.**

### The 6 core assets to make first
1. One **60-sec demo video** (the hero asset — reuse everywhere).
2. A **3-slide carousel**: Pain → Product → CTA.
3. A **one-pager** (PDF) for agencies: what it does + screenshots + "book a demo."
4. **5 short clips**, one per feature (search, pitch, marketing, Today, dashboard).
5. A **waitlist / "request access" form** (Google Form or Tally — agency, # listings, biggest pain, WhatsApp).
6. A **personal Loom** (5 min) you can DM to any interested broker.

### Where to post
- **LinkedIn** (primary — agency owners live here).
- **Instagram** (reels + carousels — agents browse here).
- **Dubai broker WhatsApp/Telegram groups** (huge in Dubai — direct reach).
- **X/Twitter** (build-in-public).
- **TikTok** (short demos can pop).

---

## 3. Recruiting beta brokers (get the first 10–20)

**Goal:** 10–20 agents actively trying it. Quality > quantity. Go direct — don't wait for inbound.

### Where to find them
- Your own network + referrals ("who do you know in real estate?").
- LinkedIn search: "Property Consultant Dubai", "Real Estate Agent Dubai".
- Bayut/Property Finder agent profiles → find them on LinkedIn/Instagram.
- Dubai real-estate WhatsApp/Telegram groups, broker meetups, RERA events.

### Outreach script — LinkedIn/WhatsApp DM
> Hi [Name] — I built an AI assistant for Dubai agents that finds matching listings, writes
> client pitches (WhatsApp/email), and creates marketing in seconds. I'm giving a few agents
> **free early access** in exchange for honest feedback. Can I send you a 60-sec demo? 🇦🇪

### Outreach script — warmer / referral
> Hey [Name], I'm building a tool to automate the boring parts of a broker's day —
> follow-ups, pitches, marketing. You know the grind better than me. Would you try it free
> and tell me what's useful and what's missing? 10 mins, and I'll set it up for you.

### The offer (make it irresistible for early testers)
- **Free** during beta + **founding-user discount for life**.
- You **set it up for them** (white-glove) — zero effort on their side.
- "Shape the product" — their feedback becomes features.

## 4. Onboarding flow (signup → active user)

Keep friction near zero. The first session must produce a **wow** within 5 minutes.

1. **Qualify (1 min):** short form — agency, # of listings, #1 time-waster.
2. **Set up for them:** create their account, import their listings (CSV/Bayut), add 2–3 of their real leads.
3. **Guided first session (15 min call or Loom):** walk them through ONE end-to-end win —
   open a lead → **Match → Pitch → Marketing**. Let them feel "it just wrote my WhatsApp."
4. **Give a 1-page quick-start** (below) + your WhatsApp for help.
5. **Define their "aha":** e.g. "send 3 AI pitches this week." Get them to do the core action fast.

### Quick-start message to send each tester
> Welcome! 3 things to try first:
> 1. **AI Search** — type what a client wants, get ranked listings.
> 2. **Open a lead → Match → Pitch** — it writes the WhatsApp message for you.
> 3. **Today** — your daily follow-up list so no lead goes cold.
> Login: [URL] · [email/password]. Stuck? WhatsApp me anytime. 🙌

---

## 5. Feedback loop (what to ask + measure)

**Track (per tester):** Did they log in again? Did they generate a pitch/marketing? Which
features did they actually use? Drop-off point?

**Weekly 3-question check-in (WhatsApp):**
1. What did you use it for this week?
2. What saved you the most time?
3. What's missing or annoying?

**After ~2 weeks, ask:**
- "On a scale of 1–10, how disappointed would you be if this disappeared?" (aim for many 8–10s).
- "Would you pay for this? What's fair per month?"
- "Can I get a 1-line testimonial / who else should try it?"

---

## 6. Turn testers into growth

- **Testimonials** → put on the landing page + posts (social proof sells the next broker).
- **Referrals** → "know 2 agents who'd want this?" Give referral perks.
- **Case study** → "Agent X sent 30 AI pitches in a week and booked 4 viewings."
- **Convert to paid** once they're hooked + you've added must-have features.

---

## 7. First 4 weeks — content + onboarding calendar

| Week | Content (post 2–3×) | Onboarding |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch video + pain post + carousel | DM 20–30 agents; onboard first 3–5 |
| 2 | Feature clips (search, pitch) + "build in public" update | Onboard to 10; first feedback calls |
| 3 | Before/after + first tester reaction/testimonial | Ship top-requested fix; re-engage quiet testers |
| 4 | Case study + "spots open" CTA | Ask for testimonials + referrals; start a waitlist |

**North-star for the test phase:** 10+ agents who'd be "very disappointed" without it, and
2–3 strong testimonials. That's your proof to scale (and to charge).

---

## 8. Common objections + answers

- *"Is my data safe?"* → Each agency's data is isolated; you're not sharing their leads.
- *"I don't have time to learn a tool."* → You set it up; it works inside WhatsApp/Telegram too.
- *"AI pitches sound robotic."* → It writes in the client's language + your style; you edit before sending.
- *"We already use a CRM."* → This isn't just a CRM — it *writes the pitch and marketing* for you.
